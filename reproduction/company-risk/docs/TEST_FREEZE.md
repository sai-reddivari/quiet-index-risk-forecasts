# Evaluation specification frozen after characteristic inspection

Frozen 14 October 2026, before running any following-quarter evaluations under the new specification. These periods were reserved for this specification, not wholly unseen in prior work.

The integrated 504-session panel has 500 issuers at each of 36 formations and retains missing characteristics separately. Descriptive inspection used `results/licensed/panel/{coverage,correlations,distributions,summaries}_504.csv` and `results/factor_exposures/formation_summary_504.csv`. It showed strong size/liquidity and ordinary/downside-beta overlap, and a small median fitted R-squared increment from SMH. This is not evidence of forecast improvement. The three primary questions and the signed SMH loading are unchanged.

Both the 504/400 and 252/200 windows are evaluated. Complete factor-dependent outcomes are the 34 quarters from 2018 Q1 through 2026 Q2, split into 20 development, 8 validation and 6 confirmation quarters. June and September 2026 formations remain descriptive. Inference averages paired quarter statistics, with Newey-West lag four and a moving block bootstrap of length four, 2,000 replications and seed 20261014. Confidence intervals are conditional on estimated historical loadings; they do not rerun first-stage estimation. Six confirmation quarters offer weak independent evidence.

Stock outcomes are log annualized following-quarter total volatility and log annualized FF5-plus-momentum residual volatility. Residuals use saved formation coefficients, never quarter refits. At least 40 valid outcome sessions are required. The baseline includes log capitalization, sector, market loading, own momentum and trailing residual volatility. Comparisons add standard style loadings, then SMH, then liquidity separately or the one predefined log-size × SMH interaction. Every paired comparison uses identical companies. Sector and GICS industry-group versions and a no-size principal comparison are reported. Sparse industry groups use the specification's five-company threshold and sector remainders.

Forward associations are fitted after outcomes and labeled as such. Forecasts use pooled expanding OLS after at least eight completed training quarters, only earlier completed outcomes, and train-only centering/scaling. Forecast category/group mappings are learned from training companies; unknown evaluation categories are counted. MSE of log volatility is the forecast loss. No clipping, tuning or outcome-driven variable selection is introduced. Quarter scores receive equal weight in inference.

The main separate liquidity extension uses log Amihud; log dollar volume is exploratory. The signed size interaction is log capitalization times conditional SMH loading, not an absolute-loading interaction chosen after results. Exploratory sample sensitivities exclude (a) the semiconductor sub-industry and (b) DHR, KDP, BKR, GEN, OXY, TKO and ECHO, whose distributions raised convention concerns. They do not redefine SMH itself or mix additive and vendor-adjusted returns.

Covariance portfolios are formed using eligible securities and fixed snapshot or equal target weights as specified, preserving both share classes. Size groups come from one issuer cap mapped to both classes; high/low SMH groups use formation loadings. Holdings are chosen before future returns. Each portfolio uses common complete training dates for all its members and both competing models, retaining the same 400/200 minimum. Both models are refitted on that common mask for the portfolio covariance exercise; these fits are distinct from the security-level measurement coefficients. No security is removed to improve future coverage. Future returns require all fixed members on the date, with omissions recorded and at least 40 days for realized variance. Fixed target weights imply daily rebalancing, not buy-and-hold drift.

Factor covariance and residual covariance use the same sample denominator, n−1. Predictions add the weighted factor variance to either diagonal residual variance or within-industry-group residual covariance, keeping that treatment identical across models. This avoids a degree-of-freedom change masquerading as incremental SMH benefit. Report predicted/realized variance, bias, log ratios, QLIKE and underestimation by period, with paired quarter uncertainty. Industry-residual results are necessary to judge whether SMH only compensates for a diagonal baseline.

Pre-evaluation reviewer amendment: the primary signed loading measures a directional relation, while factor variance depends on squared loadings. One exploratory no-size sector comparison adds the annualized conditional SMH variance component, loading squared times residualized-SMH annualized volatility squared, to the standard-styles model. This is registered before any outcome run; it does not replace the primary signed tests.

Calendar implementation clarification before evaluation: a training quarter is complete at its last observed trading session. Compare that session with the current formation close, retaining the calendar quarter-end only as a label. This avoids needlessly delaying training when the calendar quarter ends on a weekend. It does not claim that the frozen factors were publicly available then.
